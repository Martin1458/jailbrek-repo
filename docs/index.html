fastMessaging 0.1.0

Hold the right side of a DM to speed up the typing indicator

Instagram plays a reel at 2x while you hold the right side of the screen. fastMessaging brings the same gesture to Direct: when the other person is typing, press and hold the right side of the thread and the three dots run faster for as long as you hold.

It starts at 2x and doubles every 1.5 seconds you keep holding, up to 16x. The speeds, the width of the strip and the hold delay are all in Settings.

The hold only engages while someone is actually typing, so Instagram's own press and hold, the chat theme picker and message reactions, behaves exactly as it always did the rest of the time.

twoFCrop 1.1.0

Drag two crop handles at once

iOS lets you move one crop handle at a time. twoFCrop lets you drag two, so you can frame a crop in a single gesture instead of nudging each side in turn.

Works in the Photos editor and in the editor that opens when you tap the thumbnail after taking a screenshot. Any two handles can be used together: opposite corners, adjacent corners, opposite edges, or an edge and a corner.
