twoFCrop 1.0.0

Drag two crop handles at once

iOS lets you move one crop handle at a time. twoFCrop lets you drag two, so you can frame a crop in a single gesture instead of nudging each side in turn.

Works in the Photos editor and in the editor that opens when you tap the thumbnail after taking a screenshot. Any two handles can be used together: opposite corners, adjacent corners, opposite edges, or an edge and a corner.
